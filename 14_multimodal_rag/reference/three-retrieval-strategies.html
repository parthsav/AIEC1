Three ways to find a picture with words

The A/B/C strategies, their weak spots, the RRF formula, and the words that matter — on one page.

The three strategies

A · Search the catalog cards

VLM writes a text card per image (once); everything lives in one text collection.

Wins: simplest; strong for text-heavy corpora.
Weak spot: caption bottleneck — if the card missed it, it's unfindable.
Notebook: Cell 5.1

B · One shared CLIP map

CLIP embeds sentences and raw pixels into one space; a question matches images directly.

Wins: no captions needed; finds images by their pixels.
Weak spots: modality gap; can't read values off charts.
Notebook: Cells 5.2–5.3

C · Two stores, merged by rank

Text store (best text embedder) + image store (CLIP); ask both, fuse with RRF.

Wins: each modality gets its specialist; the practical default.
Weak spot: most moving parts; two searches per query.
Notebook: Cell 5.4
The rule that pairs with all three Whatever strategy finds the image, the VLM answers from the real pixels (re-loaded via source_path, Cell 7.1). Cards and CLIP find; only eyes read.

RRF in one box

Formulascore(item) = Σ over lists: 1 / (60 + rank) — then sort descending. Rank starts at 1; the 60 stops rank #1 from crushing #2.
Why ranks, not scoresDifferent embedders score on incomparable scales (two judges, two rulers). Placements are always fair.
What risesItems appearing in both lists sum contributions — agreement is the strongest signal.
SourceCormack, Clarke & Buettcher 2009 (2 pages)

Words that matter

VLM (Vision-Language Model)
A chat model that accepts images inside its messages. Does two jobs: archivist (parse at ingestion) and expert reader (read pixels at answer time).
Catalog card (caption)
The structured text a VLM writes about an image at ingestion — embedded like any text chunk so the image can be found.
CLIP
A model that embeds text and images into one shared meaning map, trained on 400M image–caption pairs. Finds; cannot read.
Modality gap
In CLIP's space, text clusters near text and images near images — so mixed collections rank text above the best image. Fix: filter per modality, or separate stores + RRF.
Caption bottleneck
Strategy A's failure mode: retrieval can only match what the card happened to mention.
RRF (Reciprocal Rank Fusion)
Merging ranked lists by summing 1/(60+rank) per item — placements, never raw scores.
recall@k
The share of test questions where a correct chunk appears in the top k retrieved.
Gold set
The answer key: question → the chunk id(s) that should come back.
Keyframe
A sampled video frame standing in for its few seconds of footage.
